Project Scope

Building a light weight software using Supabase + Angular software which will auto generate ticket based inquiries
for companies which are low to medium enterprise, Maybe add AI into the mix to maybe sort of give progress to the
user's inquiry. Also adding a better side of email automation flows by using the user's inquiry to drive but the system generates it into sort of one object or inquiry whether from WhatsApp,email,website form they go to a collection and then into a communication path.

Problem Statement

The core insight here is this: most companies start with a simple shared email address — support@company.com in a Google Group or Outlook Shared Mailbox. For a while it works. But then email volume grows and suddenly emails get missed, two people reply to the same customer with different answers, and no one knows who's handling what. Your system solves exactly that breaking point.

The Adoption Landscape among SA SMEsSouth Africa's roughly 2.6 million SMEs generally fall into two categories when it comes to adopting ticketing systems:The Lean "Micro" Majority (Low Adoption): Roughly 85% of SA small businesses operate as solo entrepreneurs or in tiny teams of fewer than 5 people. In this segment, ticketing systems are rarely adopted. These businesses heavily rely on shared spreadsheets, WhatsApp Business, and direct phone calls because formal ticketing systems are perceived as too complex or expensive

The Formal / Scaling SMEs (High Adoption): The remaining 37% of formalised, scaling SMEs—particularly those in e-commerce, retail, and IT services—are aggressively adopting tools like Zendesk, Freshdesk, and local variants (e.g., Bidvest Data Omnichannel Support). With on 37% adopting such there is still a market for such tools to be widely used in the field of business.

Effectiveness and Business 

ImpactAccording to data from the Small Enterprise Development Agency (SEDA), nearly 80% of successful SA SMEs attribute their growth directly to integrating technology into their business models. For those who take the leap into modern ticketing software, the effectiveness manifests in several distinct areas:

Drastic Cost Reductions: South African SMEs face volatile operating costs due to infrastructure challenges (like historical load shedding and currency fluctuations). Ticketing automation acts as a digital force multiplier, allowing a team of 2 people to easily manage thousands of inquiries without hiring more staff.

Seamless Omnichannel Journeys: Instead of losing a customer query across email, Instagram DM, and SMS, systems unify communication into a single journey. Local businesses report faster resolution times because agents don't waste time asking customers to repeat themselves.

POPIA Compliance: South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) penalises businesses that mishandle customer data. Ticketing systems ensure all sensitive customer complaints and data are encrypted and securely stored, mitigating massive compliance risks.

Improved Retention Rates: In a highly competitive economic climate where local consumer disposable income is under heavy pressure, keeping an existing customer is crucial. Transitioning from messy shared inboxes to clean ticketing queues yields a dramatic boost in customer satisfaction metrics.

Core Barriers to Effectiveness

While highly effective once operational, South African SMEs often face localized friction when adopting these systems:The "Fintech First" Bias: SA businesses prioritize digital payment adoption over operational software. While 90% of SA SMEs accept digital payments, far fewer prioritize backend tools like CRM or ticketing apps.Digital Infrastructure & Cost: High international software pricing (often USD-denominated) coupled with regional digital infrastructure gaps can act as a financial barrier for smaller local setups
